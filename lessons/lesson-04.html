Your First Letters: ཀ་ཁ་ག་ང་

Row 1 of the Ucen alphabet — all produced at the back of the throat. Take your time; there's no rush to Row 2 until these feel familiar.

ཀ

ka

A sharp, unaspirated "k" — like the "k" in "skate" (no puff of air), not the "k" in "kite."

Letter sound

ཀ་བ — ka-wa — pillar

Example word
ཁ

kha

An aspirated "k" — like the "k" in "kite," with a clear puff of air. Say it next to ka above and notice the difference in breath.

Letter sound

ཁ — kha — mouth

Example word
ག

ga

A soft, voiced "g" — like the "g" in "go."

Letter sound

གོང་ — gong — price

Example word
ང

nga

The tricky one — it's the "ng" sound in "sing," but used at the start of a syllable, which doesn't happen in English. Say "singer" slowly, then isolate just the "ng."

Letter sound

ང་ — nga — I / me

Example word

Practice tip

Say all four in a row, slowly: ka — kha — ga — nga. Your throat is doing very similar work each time, just changing breath and voicing. Once your mouth learns this pattern, Rows 2–4 will feel far more familiar, since they follow the same aspirated / unaspirated / voiced / nasal pattern, just moving forward in the mouth.
