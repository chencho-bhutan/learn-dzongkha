About this blog

Edit this page with your own story — where you grew up, your connection to Dzongkha, and why you started teaching it here. Readers connect with a real person behind a language blog far more than a faceless resource.

A starting point, adapted from the first post:

I'm building this blog because I believe language is one of the clearest threads connecting us to home, to family, and to a way of seeing the world that doesn't translate cleanly into English. I'm not a professional linguist — I'm someone who cares about Dzongkha staying alive and accessible, and who wanted to build the resource I wish had existed when I was trying to learn.

A personal project to keep Dzongkha alive and accessible.
